# Fragmented Terms

These words show up everywhere. They are useful. They are also slippery.

Use them carefully. If precision matters, fall back to canon terms.

## Commonly Fragmented

`agent`
: Often used to mean worker, assistant, runtime, or persona. Too broad to trust without context.

`sub-agent`
: Usually means a spawned worker, but some systems use it for a role inside the same worker.

`role`
: May mean persona, job title, lens, permission level, or output stance.

`profile`
: Sometimes a persona. Sometimes a model preset. Sometimes a whole execution template.

`session`
: May refer to a conversation, a worker lifetime, a task boundary, or a context window.

`context`
: Can mean visible text, retrieved material, conversation history, memory, or active instruction scope.

`runtime`
: Sometimes equivalent to harness. Sometimes only the lower execution layer.

`system prompt`
: Useful in platform-specific discussion, but not a canonical Caret^ term because not every harness exposes one directly.

`tool`
: May mean a callable function, an external service, or a reusable skill-like artifact.

## Repo Rule

When these terms appear in outward-facing docs, either:

- ground them with a canon term nearby, or
- swap them out for the canon term directly.
