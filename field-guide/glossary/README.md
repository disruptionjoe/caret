# Glossary

A practical vocabulary for agentic systems. Not a dictionary. A field guide.

The problem: every platform uses the same words differently. "Tool" means one thing in Claude Code, another in LangChain, another in AutoGen. "Agent" means everything from a chat wrapper to a fully autonomous system.

This glossary maps the terrain. Where terms have converged, we document the consensus. Where they haven't, we document the fragmentation.

## Structure

- **canonical.md** — Locked definitions. Terms where usage has converged.
- **competing.md** — Same concept, different names across platforms. Documents the overlap.
- **emerging.md** — Patterns that appear real but lack enough evidence to canonize.

## What moves a term from emerging to canonical?

Independent usage across at least three ecosystems, consistent meaning, and no better competing term. Maintainer call, informed by community evidence.
